## Disambiguation

The AEGENT project at `aegent.org` is distinct from:

- the DoraHacks project titled AEGENT that links to `aegent-labs/aegent` and `aegent.vercel.app`;
- AGNT, Inc. and the Nasdaq stock symbol `AGNT`;
- the `agnt.com` talent-booking marketplace;
- the `agnt-gg/agnt` software project;
- Aegent Technologies, Aegent Energy Advisors, Aegent Dev, AGNT Hub, and other similarly named organizations or products;
- the common words **AGENT** and **ARGENT**.

Those names and projects are not canonical records for the AEGENT project described by `aegent.org`, and no affiliation is implied.
